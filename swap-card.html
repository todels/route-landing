Route landing · hero swap card

Live quotes and private mode

Two things Micah asked for on the hero swap. Both are options on the same card, so the resting card stays as designed: two small switches in its header, everything else only appears when they're turned on.

1 · Live quotes

"no ui changes just one btn ... it'll just change from polling quotes to live-streaming them"

What it means: today the card asks Route for a fresh price every few seconds (polling), so the number jumps now and then and shows a refresh countdown. With Live on, Route pushes a new price to the card every block, so the number you'd get updates continuously.

On the card: one switch in the header. When it's on, the refresh countdown is replaced by a "Live" dot, and the "you get" number ticks as prices move. Nothing else changes.

2 · Private swaps, transfers, shielded addresses

"add them as an option for swaps on the landing page since we need to add them for Umbra"

What it means: Umbra is a privacy layer live on Robinhood Chain. You keep a private balance, and swaps or sends made from it hide the amounts and addresses onchain. Route is adding Umbra as an option.

On the card: a Private switch. Swaps then run through Umbra from your private balance. Two tabs appear next to Swap: Send (a private transfer to someone's private account or to a normal wallet) and Receive (your shielded address: the private address people send to).

Try it
  1. Flip Live: the "you get" number starts ticking every block.
  2. Flip Private: the card turns into the private version and Send / Receive appear.
  3. Open Send and Receive.
Colours for private mode (indigo) are placeholders to make the state obvious. The designer picks the real treatment; it just has to read as "you're in private mode" at a glance.
