02 How it worksEvery token is a node; the pools between two tokens are drawn as one bundle of strands (one strand per pool), so 3 or 30 pools stay tidy. Example ETH to PONS with USDG as the usual hop. Scan: bundles fan open while pools are read and counted, then close. Split: the best 7 of 20 strands light up, 40% direct and 60% through USDG. Check: picked pools ticked and simulated. Sign: one transaction lands in PONS. Pool counts and split are illustrative.
