How Route works

One swap.
Every pool checked.

You pick the pair. Route does the searching, splitting and checking, so you get more out of every trade.

pool names are placeholders
1Scan

Reads every pool on the chain, live. Onchain liquidity only.

2Split

Splits the trade across pools and hops for the most out, after gas.

3Check

Simulates the route through the executor before quoting.

4Sign

One transaction. Approve and swap batched where supported.
