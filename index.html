02 How it worksSteps on the left drive the isometric scene on the right; it auto-advances, click a step to jump. Scan: a beam sweeps the pools and each one flashes its price. Split: the trade arcs into the three best pools (line weight = share). Check: ticks on each pool and a dashed box around the route for the simulation. Sign: it all flows into the output with one signature. Motion idea only; final illustration to be designed.
